# Store Improvement Sprint — Instagram Design Brief

Created with built-in image generation. The source website supplied the burgundy, ivory, charcoal and rose palette and sans-serif/italic-serif typography direction. This is a raster post; no website files were modified.

## Final generation prompt

Use case: ads-marketing.
Create ONE finished, high-end Instagram feed graphic for Faysal Studio's $200 Store Improvement Sprint. This is the actual publishable flat artwork, NOT a photo of a poster, a presentation board, or a device mockup. Portrait 4:5 aspect ratio, ideally 1080 x 1350 pixels or a higher-resolution equivalent. English copy. Target audience: owners of existing ecommerce stores, not shoppers buying products.

Brand direction derived from the existing Faysal Studio website: deep burgundy #64292f, near-black wine #351418 and #121110, warm ivory #f3eee6, muted rose #d89ba2. Sophisticated editorial typography, contemporary clean sans-serif akin to Geist, with a single elegant italic serif emphasis on the word "easier". Tasteful glassmorphism, a subtle warm burgundy atmospheric gradient, restrained grain, translucent glass with soft luminous edges. Confident and premium, not a cheap discount ad. Let typography carry the message; avoid clutter and stock imagery.

Composition: generous 70px safe margins on an approximately 1080px canvas, clear left-aligned grid. Small clean text-only FAYSAL STUDIO masthead at top; do not invent a logo symbol. An oversized beautifully typeset headline is the dominant upper element, with ample negative space:
"Make your store"
"easier to buy from."
Arrange on two or three lines as needed, keeping all words.

Below it, a single large frosted-glass offer panel with a subtle warm border. The offer title and price are prominent, with "$200" very large and "ONE-TIME · FIXED SCOPE" directly beside or under the price. Panel includes five concise benefit lines, each with a tiny restrained outlined check or simple numbered marker. Keep type at least around 30px at 1080px-wide equivalent, no tiny dense body copy.

EXACT text to render, verbatim, no extra copy:
"FAYSAL STUDIO"
"Make your store"
"easier to buy from."
"Store Improvement Sprint"
"$200"
"ONE-TIME · FIXED SCOPE"
"20-min discovery call"
"Mobile shopping journey review"
"3 prioritised findings"
"1 agreed small improvement"
"Short handover"
"Scope confirmed before payment."
'DM "SPRINT" to get started'
"faysalstudio.com"

At the bottom, outside the offer panel, a strong ivory pill or clean highlighted CTA reading exactly DM "SPRINT" to get started. Website below in small but readable ivory. The scope qualifier must be legible, not concealed.
All text must be perfectly spelled, high contrast and readable at phone size. Ensure no collisions, no cropped text and no perspective distortion.
No other prices, no fabricated testimonials, no guaranteed sales increase, no percentages, no urgency, no "most popular", no fake before-and-after analytics, no promise of a full redesign or brand book, no extra logos, no watermark. Deliver only the single finished graphic.
