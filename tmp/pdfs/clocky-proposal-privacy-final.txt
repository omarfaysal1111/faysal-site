06 / THE RED-CARPET EXPERIENCE




A watch buyer should feel guided,
not left to search.
Watches are a considered, niche purchase. The site should feel like a private showroom: calm, clear and
reassuring at every step.




 01    Arrival
       A distinct Arabic and English welcome, written naturally for each customer.




 02    Curated showroom
       Browse by style, occasion, budget and product type - with less noise and clearer direction.




 03    Shoppable watch story
       Every video names the watch and opens its exact product page through a clear 'Shop this watch' action.




 04    Confidence lounge
       Product type, specifications, close-ups, customer proof, delivery, returns and warranty sit beside the
       decision.
                                                                                                                THE EXPERIENCE PRINCIPLE


                                                                                                                One watch. One story.
 05    Concierge checkout                                                                                       One effortless next step.
       A simple checkout and visible WhatsApp support make the final step feel personal and effortless.
